instructions identifying its neighborhoods, must-visits, intercity transfers,

using only this outline, so define explicit boundaries for every day:

- end_location is the CITY name where the day finishes. Use concise city labels,

- overnight_base names the assumed sleeping base, or explains the final-day departure.
- incoming_transfer is null if no intercity transfer is needed, or contains the
  origin, destination, mode, realistic duration and route notes. Put intercity
  transfers on their arrival day, never on two days. Schedule enough time for them.
State exact shared place labels, hotel-base assumptions, and must-visits in the
instructions so independently generated schedules connect. Avoid overnight
transfers that require a previous generated schedule; explain any necessary exception.
Respect the user's pace, constraints, whole-group budget, and realistic travel time.
Include trip-wide assumptions, estimated exchange rates when needed, and warnings.
